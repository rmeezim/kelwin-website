## 1 · What we are (your yes: DECISIONS.md, decision 11)

> Kelwin is a GTM systems partner for B2B companies. We build and run the system behind what their buyers hear, whom
> they approach and why their deals close. It lives in their own accounts and documents.

## 2 · Who we serve

- **The reader:** the CEO or founder, head of sales or CFO of a B2B company.
- **The company:** paying customers, buying committees, and revenue that is real but hard to predict a quarter
  ahead. Often the founder still closes most deals.
- **One clear problem:** a fixed-scope engagement (the offer work's proposed name for the smaller family).
- **Not for:** companies before revenue, products bought at a checkout, and list buyers.

## 3 · The problem, as buyers tell it

Notes for writers, not quotes. They never go on a page in quote marks, where a reader would take them as testimony.
- They can't predict next quarter from this one.
- Their champion loves them, and then the CFO says no.
- The founder still writes every important email.
- Each quarter starts from zero.

Our reading:

> Revenue is an architectural problem. What buyers hear, whom you approach and why deals are lost are decided
> separately, so a fix in one doesn't last.

## 4 · What we build

| Capability | The line | Assets |
|---|---|---|
| Narrative Systems | We work out what each kind of buyer needs to hear from you, starting from your buyers' own words. | Narrative OS, Channel Playbooks |
| GTM Infrastructure | We find which companies to approach, and when, and build that choice into your CRM and its rules. | ICP & Signal Map, Orchestration Rules |
| GTM Intelligence | We trace why your deals are won, lost or stalled, and each quarter we rank what to change next. | Signal Dashboard, Rebuild Sequence |

Outreach goes only to accounts on the ICP & Signal Map. It is described on Engagements, never in a headline.

## 5 · How it runs

- **The way in is the System Audit:** a 30-minute first call with the founding team, ten working days on your CRM
  and deal notes, then the readout, live on a 60-minute call. No fee; the audit page says so once.
- **Launch, Growth and Scaling are the retained engagements** (the offer work's proposed name; DECISIONS.md,
  decision 1).
  > We build the system in your own accounts and run it. Your leadership decides what changes each quarter.

  Six months recommended, the first three a pilot that ends at day 90 with the first Rebuild Sequence. The pilot's
  lower fee is said on the first call and in the proposal, never on the site.
- **The fixed-scope engagements are the other way in.**
  > When one part of the system matters most now, we take that part on by itself, with the same method.
- **The pilot's meeting fee, for first calls and proposals only** (settled with the offer: the fee itself is
  DECISIONS.md, decision 4). Every proposal shows the audit's
  findings and the six assets before any fee. The fee is then said in one sentence:
  > In the pilot, part of our fee is paid for each first meeting our outreach creates and that takes place with an
  > account on the agreed ICP & Signal Map. It shares the risk while the system is new, and it stops when the pilot
  > ends.

  Never "per lead". Meeting fees end with month 3 on every way to start, and month to month has none, so the sentence
  holds. It matches the contract's "held meeting": our outreach made the first contact.
- Terms, rhythms, who sends and the exit go on Engagements and in the proposal, never on a first screen.

## 6 · Why us (true today)

1. **Method:** Diagnose, Architect, Install, Calibrate. Nothing is built before the readout ranks what holds revenue
   back.
2. **A point of view (your yes):** a line stays in Narrative OS only while buyers' evidence supports it. "Each
   quarter we bring your leadership the lines the evidence no longer supports, favorites included."
3. **Six assets,** each shown as a specimen with our change on it.
4. **Where it lives:** "The system lives in your own accounts and documents." Once a page at most.
5. **Who is in the room (your yes: DECISIONS.md, decision 6):** "The founding team presents your readout, and is in
   the room at the kickoff, at day 90 and at each renewal." The same sentence everywhere. Proposals name every seat by
   role, marked founding team or contractor.
6. **Selective:** the audit is a gate. "If we're not the fix, the readout says so, and names what would be."

## 7 · Proof

**Today:** the homepage's four sourced readings, labeled specimens for one invented company, three field notes and
the readout itself. Reports says: "No report is published yet. Each one needs the client's written yes."

**The plan:** a publication clause in every contract, anonymized unless the client agrees to be named; the baseline
recorded at kickoff; the first report after the first measured result; and, with your yes, asking the companies
behind your earlier audits to let us quote what the audit found.

## 8 · What we never do

- We don't buy or sell lists.
- We don't name a client without the client's written yes.
- We don't invent people, clients, logos or results, or state a team size.
- We don't publish a figure we can't source or an example we don't label.
- We don't put prices on the site.
- We don't sell a full engagement when one part is the problem.
- We don't take on two companies that compete for the same buyers at the same time **(your yes: DECISIONS.md,
  decision 10)**.
- We don't call a client's buyers unless the client introduces us **(your yes: DECISIONS.md, decision 10)**.
- We don't name competitors, or compare ourselves with other firms.

Consent rules for each channel go in the contract and the Privacy notice, not here.
