Fit

Not for everyone. We qualify first.

A revenue system only compounds inside a company built to carry it — so the gate works in both directions.

Qualification · strictWe check fit in the audit.If it isn’t there, the readout says so.

Built for — we say yes when03

  1. Growth is real, just not predictable yet. There’s a motion we can measure, then make repeatable.

  2. Founder-led or lean GTM teams. When we leave, the system stays — and it’s yours.

  3. Several people sign. What they repeat to each other decides it — not a coupon at a checkout.

Not for — we decline when03

  1. There’s no working motion to measure yet — the system has nothing to compound. Start once first revenue is in.

  2. The system assumes a sales-carried deal.

  3. Renting activity is the cycle we exist to end — a list vendor is the better call.
