A system, not a project

Phase 01 is the System Audit: ten working days, fixed scope.

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    We start by finding what’s actually broken. It’s rarely the thing you’ve been told to fix — most teams are optimising a symptom downstream of the real constraint.

    OutputThe real constraint, named — and the case for fixing it first.

  2. 02

    Architect

    Then we design the system that decides what you build and what you ignore — the category you’ll own, the language you’ll use, the model the company runs on.

    OutputYour category defined, and the operating model written down.

  3. 03

    Install

    Architecture that lives in a slide changes nothing. We wire it into how the company runs — the motions, the words people use, how a deal gets decided on a Tuesday.

    OutputA working operating layer, in use across the team.

  4. 04 · Continuous

    Calibrate

    A revenue system drifts the moment the market moves. So we stay on the signal — watching what works, retuning what slips, keeping your language ahead of the category.

    OutputA standing signal review, and a system that keeps compounding.
