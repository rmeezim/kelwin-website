Where it breaks, by market.

  • Enterprise SaaS

    The story that wins the champion dies in the committee, where every stakeholder carries a different version of what you do.

  • Cybersecurity

    Every vendor promises the same three outcomes in the same words, to buyers paid to distrust them.

  • FinTech & payments

    Compliance vocabulary smothers the actual story, and deals stall in risk review because nobody armed the champion.

  • Developer & data infrastructure

    Engineers try the product from the docs, and trust only what sounds like engineering wrote it.

  • Professional & B2B services

    Growth stops where the partners’ networks end, because nobody wrote the pitch down.

  • Industrial & deep tech

    Against legacy incumbents, twelve-month deals drift until the narrative that opened them is gone.
