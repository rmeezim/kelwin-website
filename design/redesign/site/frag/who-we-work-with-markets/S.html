Where it breaks

The same three situations, in six markets.

Committee-decided deals make all three sharper. These are the markets where they show up most.

The story that wins the champion dies in the committee, where every stakeholder carries a different version of what you do.

Every vendor promises the same three outcomes in the same words. Differentiation is a language problem before it’s a product one.

Compliance vocabulary smothers the actual story, and deals stall in risk review because nobody armed the champion.

The buyer reads docs, not decks. Outbound only works when it sounds like engineering wrote it.

Product-led with a sales-carried expansion is in scope. Card-at-checkout with no sales conversation is not.

Growth stalls where the partners’ networks end — the firm’s knowledge never became a sellable narrative.

Twelve-month deals drift: the narrative that opened the deal isn’t the one that closes it.
