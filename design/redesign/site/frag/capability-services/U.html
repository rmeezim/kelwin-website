The questions it answers

  1. Which signals come before revenue?

    Signal taxonomy & intent monitoring. Your closed-won deals, traced back to the first thing that changed at each account.

  2. Why do we really win and lose?

    Win/loss & objection intelligence. Calls with the buyers who decided, written up in their words, not the rep’s.

  3. Which version of the message is landing?

    Narrative resonance telemetry. Reply quality and meetings booked, by message version. Opens are not counted.

  4. What changes next quarter?

    Quarterly calibration program. The other three answers, weighed with your leadership into one ranked list of what to build, keep and cut.
