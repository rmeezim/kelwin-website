What every seat is held to

Four rules, and the deal that comes with them.

The rules

  1. H·01

    You own systems, not tasks.

    Everyone is accountable to the thing the engagement leaves behind — not to hours logged against it.

  2. H·02

    Writing is the job.

    If it isn’t written down in the client’s language, it didn’t happen.

  3. H·03

    Diagnose out loud.

    We show clients our reasoning, including the uncertain parts. The same rule applies inside the firm.

  4. H·04

    No theater.

    No status meetings that could be a readout, no decks that could be a page, no busyness worn as a badge.
