The scope

Everything we change sits in your sending setup.

In the scope

  • Your existing domains and sending tools, set up with your admin
  • SPF, DKIM and DMARC aligned on every sending domain
  • Outreach moved to its own domains, aged before use
  • Unsubscribes and suppression honored in every tool
  • Placement monitoring, live in your stack

Not in the scope

  • Writing or sending campaigns
  • Buying or cleaning lists
  • Inbox rotation and warmup services
  • A promise of placement: mailbox providers decide it
