Deliverability is infrastructure.

Before your message can be judged, it has to exist. For a growing share of B2B outbound, it doesn’t — it dies in a filter, unseen, while the dashboard reports it as ‘delivered.’ Deliverability isn’t a growth hack. It’s the load-bearing floor under every channel decision you make.
