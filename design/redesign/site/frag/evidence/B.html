03 Evidence

Four numbers. One diagnosis.

None of them is a pipeline problem.Every figure is sourced. None of them is ours.

  1. 17%

    1 tick = 1 in 100 →

    N·01 · Visibility

    Buyers spend just 17% of the journey with sales.

    The deal is decided by your narrative — not your reps.

    The decision is mostly made before a rep is in the room, on what the market already believes about you. That is narrative work, not more outreach.

    Gartner · B2B buying journey

  2. 95:5

    95 of 100 · not in market today5 · buying

    N·02 · Timing

    95% of your market isn’t buying right now.

    You win by being remembered when they are — not by chasing harder.

    At any moment only about 5% of your market is actively buying. You can’t convert the 95% who aren’t ready; you can only be the name they recall when they are. That is mental availability, not pipeline volume.

    See how we diagnose it

    Ehrenberg-Bass · the 95:5 rule

  3. 77%

    N·03 · Complexity

    77% of buyers say the purchase was hard to make.

    The friction is your buying process — make deciding easier and you win.

    Too many stakeholders and no clear path, not reps underperforming. You win by making the decision easier to make.

    Gartner · B2B buying survey

  4. 53%

    N·04 · Experience

    53% of loyalty comes from the buying experience.

    How you sell outweighs brand, product and price combined.

    How you frame and guide the decision outweighs everything sitting in your pipeline. The way you sell is the product.

    CEB · Harvard Business Review
