Message matrix

Filled in for Kelwin itself, since no client’s matrix can be shown.

The message matrix: three listeners, and for each their problem, what we do about it, what proves it, and what happens if it goes wrong.
Listener Their problem What we do about it What proves it If it goes wrong
Championusually the founder or CEO Pipeline still runs through the founder and stalls when they step back. We find the one constraint, then build the fix in their own tools. A sample readout and the field notes, before any call. They keep the readout and every document we wrote.
Economic buyerthe CFO Every quarter’s pipeline costs as much to build as the last. The work goes into documents they own and carries over. The audit is scoped and agreed before any build is priced. Nothing needs a retainer to keep running.
Skepticthe head of sales The last agency sent volume, hurt a domain and left nothing. Fewer sends, routed by signal, from domains set up for it. Findings tied to records in their own CRM, which they can check. The sellers keep the talk tracks and objection answers.
