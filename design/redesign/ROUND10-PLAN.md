# Round 10 — plan (from the founder's round-9 answers; his words: ROUND9-NOTES.md)

Round 10 changes the order of work. Before any page is rebuilt again, the founder decides on four things,
each from a written proposal:

1. **The sitemap**: every page the site has, needs or should lose, each with its purpose, its part in lead
   generation and its part in how Kelwin is perceived (his ask).
2. **The business model**: the packages, the pilot, the pricing levers, the projects and what the site shows
   of prices (his model, researched and stress-tested).
3. **The artifact language**: animated, engineered drawings that make the interior pages feel alive and
   proprietary, plus a page turn that is Kelwin's own rather than Palantir's (prototypes).
4. **The voice**: a written Kelwin voice that stops the copy reading as AI-made (a guide and one page rewritten).

Then round 10 rebuilds the pages against the approved sitemap, model, artifact language and voice.

## A · Decided by the founder (binding)

### Company and offer
- **A1 Retainer first, handover on request.** Kelwin's priority is keeping clients on a retainer, for cash flow
  and to build the data for a future platform. A system is handed over only when a client asks. Everything the
  site says about handover, exits and operators must be reframed around this.
  - Proposed framing, for his yes: what we install lives in the client's own accounts and documents, so the
    client owns it and could take it; we run it.
- **A2 Engagements.** A 6-month minimum. Months 1–3 are the pilot, testing angles. Months 4–6 are the scale
  phase, putting more into what worked. Renewal is for 3–6 months, 1 year or 2 years.
  - Packages:
    - **Launch**: $8–12k a month.
    - **Growth**: $15–23k a month.
    - **Scaling**: $25–100k a month.
  - Services can be added or removed per package.
  - The pilot is at about 45% of the retainer, plus $200–300 per ICP-qualified lead and 4% of revenue from deals
    sourced in the pilot.
  - After the pilot: the full retainer, $150 per lead, and 3% revenue share capped at $50k (or $100k).
  - An exit after the pilot on good-faith grounds is undecided; he wants our opinion.
  - He wants deep research on whether the model is competitive, what each package includes, and its cadence and
    maximum scope.
- **A3 Projects.**
  - Launch the six; hold Sales narrative.
  - Each project can also run on a retainer, and projects are an upsell path into the packages.
  - Prices stay off the site for now. Project prices would start around $3,500–4,000 a month, and he wants
    our advice on whether and how to show prices.
  - The week-one stop and the 30-day check, the tools and data held in the client's name, and where projects
    appear are all as proposed.
- **A4 Audit.**
  - Free.
  - One first call, 30 minutes.
  - The readout is presented on the call, not sent ahead.
  - Approved as built (P09).
- **A5 Legal identity.**
  - No "LLC" anywhere: just "Kelwin". The company is registered in Delaware, with a virtual office in
    Midtown Manhattan.
  - Address as shown on the site: "New York, New York, USA".
  - Client data is taken out of Privacy (the Lab data line goes).
- **A6 Mailboxes.** audit@kelwin.co and careers@kelwin.co.
